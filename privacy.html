PLAYER PRIVACY & SAFETY

Show your game.
Keep private details private.

This plain-language guide explains what you submit and what may appear on an approved NextUp profile. Players under 18 should read it with a parent or guardian.

Information you submit

The profile form asks for your name, exact age, position and region, and optionally your club, profile photo and a YouTube or Vimeo highlight link. It also asks for a payment reference so the owner can match a manual payment to your submission.

NextUp uses these details to review submissions, verify payment references and manage player listings. Exact age and payment reference are for review and are not shown on the public player board.

What becomes public

If approved, your name, position, age range, region, club, and any submitted photo or highlight link may be shown publicly. The public listing does not show your exact age or payment reference.

Do not include a home address, school, personal phone number, private email, or other sensitive information in your profile, photo or video.

Players under 18

A parent or guardian must review and approve the profile and any media before a player under 18 submits it. The form asks the submitter to confirm this approval; the checkbox is an attestation, not an independent identity check.

Only submit photos or videos you have permission to share publicly. If you or your guardian are unsure, leave the media fields blank and ask for help first.

Payment and profile approval

The annual listing is $7.08 USD per year. The website does not process payments. Request payment instructions privately through WhatsApp, then submit your transfer reference. Payment and profile content are reviewed manually; a listing does not guarantee a trial, contract or contact from a scout.

Never send a payment PIN, password or one-time verification code to anyone.

Third-party services and requests

Supabase stores profile information and submitted photos for site operation. Highlight videos remain hosted by YouTube or Vimeo; opening those links is subject to the video provider’s terms and privacy practices.

To ask a question, correct your details or request profile removal, contact Bright Todan on WhatsApp.
